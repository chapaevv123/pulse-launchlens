# Product scope

LaunchLens has five capabilities and no more:

1. Launch discovery
2. Provenance
3. Market state
4. Executability
5. Explained RESEARCH / WATCH / SUPPRESS decisions

It is Robinhood Chain-only and read-only. Wallet connections, execution,
portfolio management, alerts, social intelligence, scoring, and multi-chain
support are explicitly out of scope for this submission.
