# Demo video script — 2:55 target

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem

**On screen:** LaunchLens hero and feed.

“A new token appearing on Robinhood Chain does not tell you whether its source
is real, whether a market exists, or whether you can get back out. LaunchLens
turns that fragmented evidence into one inspectable flow.”

## 0:15–0:35 — Launch appears

**On screen:** Point to the feed and REPLAY label.

“This feed uses genuine persisted Robinhood Chain observations. Replay keeps
the demo deterministic between launches; it follows the same UI and decision
path as the live read-only adapter.”

## 0:35–1:00 — Provenance

**On screen:** Open PUMPIT, show contract, source, block, and external links.

“Every launch starts with identity: exact token contract, observed block,
timestamp, and attributed launch source. The explorer and Pons links point to
this record—not a generic homepage.”

## 1:00–1:35 — Market state

**On screen:** Market panel and QUOTE ONLY state.

“LaunchLens separates what is known from what is not. Here a quote-derived
price exists, but aggregate liquidity and 24-hour volume do not, so the market
state says QUOTE ONLY. It never infers liquidity from a successful quote.”

## 1:35–2:05 — Executability

**On screen:** Entry, exit, benchmark size, immediate exit value, and impact.

“The engine made no trade. It recorded a standardized read-only quote: whether
an entry filled, whether an immediate exit filled, and the observed round-trip
impact. Exit evidence is as visible as entry evidence.”

## 2:05–2:30 — Decisions

**On screen:** PUMPIT RESEARCH, MORPHO WATCH, stale and unattributed SUPPRESS.

“RESEARCH means verified, fresh, and executable with acceptable observed
impact. WATCH keeps developing or expensive markets visible. SUPPRESS handles
stale launches and incomplete source identity. There is no magic score.”

## 2:30–2:50 — Replay and safety

**On screen:** Return to feed; show three decision colors.

“The replay preserves genuine event IDs, blocks, contracts, and quotes. The
public app is read-only: no wallet, no signing, no transactions, and no private
Pulse database.”

## 2:50–2:55 — Close

“Pulse LaunchLens: know what launched, whether it actually trades, and why it
deserves attention.”
