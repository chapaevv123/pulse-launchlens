# Judge story

Robinhood Chain traders and researchers see new tokens across contract events,
launchpads, and fragmented market surfaces. A launch appearing is not the same
as a launch being identifiable, liquid, or safely exit-able.

LaunchLens creates one evidence-first inspection flow: identify the contract
and source, show what market data is actually known, test a standardized
read-only entry and immediate exit path, and explain whether the result deserves
RESEARCH, should remain on WATCH, or should be SUPPRESSED.

The differentiator is not prediction. It is verifiable provenance plus exit
evidence, freshness/no-chase context, and explicit suppression when evidence is
weak. Replay makes that decision process reproducible even between launches.
