# Pitch video script — 1:45 target

“I'm an independent builder in Ukraine working on Pulse, an evidence-first
opportunity intelligence system.

On a new chain, discovering a token early is easy to confuse with discovering
a real opportunity. Launches are fragmented across contracts, launchpads, and
market feeds. A visible buy route also tells you nothing about whether you can
exit.

Pulse LaunchLens is a read-only launch intelligence terminal built specifically
for Robinhood Chain. It shows what launched, the exact contract and source,
what market evidence is genuinely available, and whether a standardized
read-only quote could both enter and immediately exit. Then it explains one of
three conservative decisions: RESEARCH, WATCH, or SUPPRESS.

Robinhood Chain is the right place for this now because its mainnet launch
ecosystem is emerging quickly. Researchers need provenance and execution
evidence before noise hardens into bad decisions.

During the hackathon I built the standalone LaunchLens product boundary: a
public-safe data contract, genuine deterministic replay, a dedicated feed and
detail experience, the explainable decision layer, and a zero-secret read-only
Cloudflare deployment. The private Pulse observation engine predates the event
and is disclosed separately.

LaunchLens does not predict winners or execute trades. Its value is simpler:
help Robinhood Chain users investigate launches faster, understand exactly
what is known, and suppress what cannot be justified.”
